Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

LuPilates collects only what it needs to show you your training schedule and remind you about it. We don't sell your data, we don't advertise in the app, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

Account information

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address and store them as your profile. We never see or store your Google password.

Training information

Your scheduled and completed sessions — dates, times, titles, and any notes studio staff add — along with your membership status.

Notification settings

Whether reminders are switched on, and how far in advance you want them.

Device token

If you allow notifications, we store an anonymous push token for your device, along with the platform (iOS or Android). This is what lets a reminder reach your phone. It identifies a device, not a person, and it is deleted along with your account.

What we don't collect

No location data. No health or fitness data from Apple Health. No contacts, photos, or camera access. No advertising identifiers. No analytics or tracking SDKs. The app requests no device permissions other than notifications, which are optional.

How we use it

  • To show you your schedule and training history
  • To send the session reminders you've asked for
  • To let studio staff manage bookings for their clients

That's the complete list. We don't use your information for anything else.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google LLC, which processes it on our behalf. Push notifications are delivered through the Expo push notification service and Apple's Push Notification service, which receive only the device token and the reminder text.

Access is restricted by security rules: you can read and write only your own profile, sessions, and settings. Studio administrators can see the schedules of the clients they manage.

Who we share it with

Nobody. We don't sell, rent, or trade your information, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. The only third parties involved are the service providers named above, who process data solely to make the app work. We may disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account with the studio. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your profile, session records, notification settings, and device tokens.

Your choices

  • Turn reminders off at any time in the app's Settings, or in iPhone Settings › Notifications.
  • Request a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your account and everything associated with it.
  • Correct anything that's wrong — email us or speak to studio staff.

Children

LuPilates is intended for studio clients and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles your information, we'll update this page and revise the date at the top.
