Common questions

I can't sign in

LuPilates uses Google Sign-In, and your account must be created by studio staff before you can use the app. If sign-in completes but you don't see your schedule, email us with the address you signed in with and we'll check your account.

I'm not getting reminders

Check two things. In the app, open Settings and confirm reminders are switched on and set to the timing you want. Then, in the iPhone Settings app, go to Notifications › LuPilates and make sure notifications are allowed. Reminders are scheduled when you open the app, so if you haven't opened it in a while, launching it once will bring your reminders up to date.

A session is wrong or missing

Sessions are scheduled by studio staff, so the app shows whatever is currently on the studio's schedule. If something looks wrong, contact the studio and it'll update in the app as soon as the change is made.

How do I change my reminder timing?

Open Settings in the app. You can choose a reminder 24 hours before a session, one hour before, or both — and turn reminders off entirely at any time.

I want my account deleted

Email us from the address associated with your account and we'll delete your profile, session records, and notification settings. See our privacy policy for what's stored.
